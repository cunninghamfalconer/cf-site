Who did these

I'm Chris Shaw. I named the studio after my grandfather, Cunningham Falconer Shaw, because it sounded better than Chris Shaw Illustration.

Designer for twenty-plus years. Grew up on a small ranch, drew my first logo at fifteen and never stopped. I led design for print shops, apparel brands and packaging, then for cities, developers and hospitality groups, and picked up creative direction and strategy along the way. Still a designer first.

Two things I do best. I take a brand off the screen and into the built world: signage, monuments, wayfinding, packaging and print. And I make a place feel real before it exists. The posters, records and flash keep my hand sharp.
